[#xactor-turns]
== Turns

A turn is one delivery: the scheduler calls an actor's `handle` with one
message and an `xactor::turn<Message>`, and the turn is everything the actor
may do to the system while it handles that message. The logic never sees the
scheduler, so every effect goes through the turn, which knows the actor that
is acting and the execution the message belongs to: a message the actor sends
carries it as its sender, and is paid from the fuel of that execution.

The turn lives for one `handle`. It holds references to the scheduler and to
the message, so a logic never keeps it, nor a reference into the message.

[cols="3,5,1",options="header"]
|===
| Call | What it does | Fuel

| `send(to, message)`
| Sends a message to an actor, itself included.
| 1

| `reply(message)`
| Sends a message to the sender of the message being handled, in its
  execution.
| 1

| `spawn_child<Logic>(inputs)`
| Constructs a `Logic` from `inputs` and runs it as a new actor of the same
  system, a child of this one. Returns its `actor_ref` (<<xactor-lifecycle>>).
| 0

| `stop_child(child)`
| Stops a child of this actor and every actor below it. Returns the actors it
  stopped (<<xactor-lifecycle>>).
| 0

| `finish(output)`
| Ends this actor with an output: it becomes `done`, and receives nothing
  more. Once only.
| 0

| `stop()`
| Ends this actor as `stopped`, alone: its children are its logic's to stop.
  Once only.
| 0

| `wake_at(deadline, message)`
| Arms a timer: the message comes back to this actor when the host's time
  reaches `deadline` (<<xactor-timers>>).
| 1

| `wake_at(deadline, message, key)`
| The same, replacing this actor's timer under `key`.
| 1

| `cancel_timer(key)`
| Removes this actor's timer under `key`, if it has one.
| 0

| `now()`
| The time the host brought last (<<xactor-timers>>).
| 0

| `spend(units)`
| Pays for work this actor does itself, all the units or none
  (<<xactor-fuel>>).
| `units`

| `fuel()`
| The fuel this execution has left.
| 0

| `self()`, `parent()`
| This actor, and the actor whose turn spawned it, none for an actor the host
  created.
| 0
|===

The quick start's logics use some of these calls
(<<xactor-quick-start>>): the counter sends its asks with `send` and ends with
`finish`, and the echo answers with `reply`, so it never needs to know who
asked. The sections that follow show the others at work: `spend` and `fuel` in
<<xactor-fuel>>, `wake_at`, `cancel_timer` and `now` in <<xactor-timers>>, and
`spawn_child` in <<xactor-lifecycle>>.

The rules of a turn:

* *A send is queued.* A message sent waits in its target's mailbox, and its
  target handles it in a turn of its own, later, in the order of the queue;
  nothing an actor sends runs inside its own `handle`.
* *A reply needs a sender.* A message from the host has none, and a reply to
  it is `invalid_argument`.
* *Every call that can fail returns a `result`,* and changes nothing when it
  fails. The logic returns the failure to end its actor with it, or goes on.
* *An address must name an actor.* A `send` to an address that names no actor
  is `invalid_argument`, and nothing is paid. A `send` to an actor that has
  ended is paid, and the message is dropped, as every message to an actor
  that has ended is (<<xactor-lifecycle>>).
* *The rest of the turn runs after `finish` or `stop`.* The actor receives
  nothing more, and in the rest of that turn it can still send, but a
  `wake_at` or a `spawn_child` is `invalid_argument`, and nothing is paid or
  constructed.
