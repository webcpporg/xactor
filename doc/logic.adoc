[#xactor-logic]
== Writing an actor logic

An actor logic is a class derived from `xactor::actor_logic<Message>` that
overrides its one pure virtual function, `handle`, which takes the actor's
turn and the envelope of the message, and returns an `xactor::result<void>`,
as the quick start's echo and counter do (<<xactor-quick-start>>).

* *Its input.* What the logic is constructed with is its input:
  `create_actor<Logic>(system, inputs)` and a turn's
  `spawn_child<Logic>(inputs)` pass their arguments to the constructor of
  `Logic`. The quick start's counter takes the echo's `actor_ref` as its
  input.
* *Its state.* Its members are the actor's state, and only its own `handle`
  changes them: the counter's sum and count of answers.
* *One message at a time.* `handle` is called once for each message, and
  returns before the next is handed over. It reads the message from
  `cause.payload`, with `std::get_if`, `std::holds_alternative` or
  `std::visit`.
* *Owned by the scheduler.* A logic can be neither copied nor moved: once it
  runs, the scheduler owns it and never moves it.
* *Errors end it.* A `handle` that returns an error ends its actor with the
  status `error`, and the scheduler keeps the error for `error_of`. The rest
  of the system goes on: every other actor keeps its turns, and the driver's
  run goes on. A handler may return any `boost::system::error_code`, of
  xactor's category or of its own; the scheduler keeps it as it is.

Every call of xactor that can fail returns an `xactor::result<T>`,
Boost.System's `result` with an `error_code`, and changes nothing when it
fails. A logic checks the result of each call: it returns a failure to end its
actor with it, as the quick start's counter does with a failed send, or it
goes on, as the relay of <<xactor-fuel>> does. `xactor::failure<T>(code)`
builds a failed `result<T>`. xactor's own codes are `xactor::errc` values, of
the category `webcpp.xactor`, and their `message()` is their name:

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
| Code | When

| `errc::invalid_argument`
| A null logic given to `spawn`; an address that names no actor, given to
  `deliver`, `send`, `stop` or `status_of`; a reply to a message from the
  host; a turn's `finish` or `stop` once its actor has ended, a second
  `finish` among them; a `stop_child` of an actor that is not the caller's
  child; a `wake_at` or a `spawn_child` once the actor has ended.

| `errc::resource_limit`
| A `send`, `reply`, `wake_at` or `spend` that the execution's fuel cannot pay
  (<<xactor-fuel>>).
|===
