[#xactor-guarantees]
== Guarantees

What xactor promises whatever its caller does: the rules its results keep,
which the sections above teach and the tests check. A guarantee keeps its
number, and the anchor named after it, so a comment of the code or of a test
cites it by that anchor: `+(doc: #xactor-invariant-13)+` sends its reader to
the thirteenth guarantee. `b2 libs/xactor/doc`, which builds this page, fails
on a citation that names no anchor of it.

The scheduler, its turns, its drivers and its log:

. [[xactor-invariant-1]]The messages of one mailbox are handled in the
order they were enqueued.
. [[xactor-invariant-2]]`run_one` takes the actor at the front of the queue
and puts it back at the end while its mailbox is not empty, so delivery is
first-in first-out across actors too.
. [[xactor-invariant-3]]An actor is in the queue at most once.
. [[xactor-invariant-4]]`run_one` handles exactly one message, and returns
`false` only when the scheduler is idle. A handler that returns an error
ends its actor with the status `error` and keeps the error for `error_of`,
unless its turn had already ended the actor, which then stays as it ended;
`run_one` still returns `true`, and every other actor keeps its turns.
. [[xactor-invariant-5]]`deliver`, `send`, `reply` and a released timer put
an active target actor in the queue when it is not there; a message to an
actor that has ended is dropped (<<xactor-invariant-23,guarantee 23>>).
. [[xactor-invariant-6]]Each `correlation_id` names one execution. The first
`deliver`, `clock_tick` or `release_next` that names it opens it with
`budgets.fuel`, and nothing refills it: a later delivery with the same
correlation shares what it has left. A delivery from the host costs no
fuel.
. [[xactor-invariant-7]]Inside a turn, `send` and `reply` cost one unit,
`wake_at`, with a key or without, one unit and `spend(n)` n units, all from
the execution of the message being handled; `spawn_child`, `stop_child`,
`stop`, `finish`, `cancel_timer`, `self`, `parent`, `now` and `fuel` cost
nothing.
. [[xactor-invariant-8]]What the execution cannot pay fails with
`resource_limit` and changes nothing: nothing is enqueued, armed or spent,
and a keyed `wake_at` leaves the timer under its key armed. `spend` takes
all its units or none.
. [[xactor-invariant-9]]`turn::fuel` is the fuel the execution of the
message being handled has left; `scheduler::fuel_of` the same from outside,
and the whole budget for an execution not yet opened.
. [[xactor-invariant-10]]`reply` sends to the sender of the message being
handled, in its execution. A message from the host has no sender, and a
reply to it is `invalid_argument`, as a `send` to an address that names no
actor is; neither is paid.
. [[xactor-invariant-11]]Timers fire only for a deadline at or before the
time the host brought, in (deadline, arming order) order. A timer replaced,
cancelled or dropped never fires; a replacement takes a new arming order.
. [[xactor-invariant-12]]No timer fires without a `clock_tick` or a
`release_next` that reaches its deadline: time never advances by itself.
. [[xactor-invariant-13]]A timer released by a `clock_tick` or a
`release_next` belongs to that call's execution, and its message's sender
is its own actor; releasing it costs nothing, since arming it was paid.
. [[xactor-invariant-14]]`run_until_idle` returns only when `idle()` is
true, and `run_turn(n)` handles at most n messages.
. [[xactor-invariant-15]]`fifo_driver` and `asio_driver` produce identical
envelope logs for the same deliveries.
. [[xactor-invariant-16]]An envelope log given to `run_one` records
{sequence, to, from, correlation, the fuel the execution had when the
message was delivered, payload index} for every message delivered, before
its actor handles it, and nothing for a dropped one.
. [[xactor-invariant-17]]Two schedulers built with the same budgets and fed
the same deliveries produce identical envelope logs.
. [[xactor-invariant-18]]xactor reads no clock, no disk and no network.
. [[xactor-invariant-19]]No turn blocks: nothing calls `io_context::run`,
`run_one` or `run_for`.
. [[xactor-invariant-20]]xactor throws no exception of its own: every
operation that can fail returns a `result`, and the library compiles without
exceptions, which the lint checks for every public header. Where exceptions
are on, an
operation that allocates, such as `spawn`, `deliver`, `send`, `wake_at` or
the log's `record`, can still throw `std::bad_alloc`, as a standard container
does.
. [[xactor-invariant-21]]There is no static mutable state: two schedulers
in one process are independent.
. [[xactor-invariant-22]]`test_driver` exposes `step`, exactly one
`run_one`, with or without a log, and nothing else; messages reach its
scheduler through `deliver`.
. [[xactor-invariant-23]]A spawned actor is active. Only an active actor
receives messages: once it is done, error or stopped, its pending messages
are dropped, and every later message to it is dropped too, never delivered
and never logged. Dropping is not an error of the sender's: a `send` to an
actor that has ended succeeds, and is paid.
. [[xactor-invariant-24]]`finish(output)` makes the actor handling the
message done and keeps the output for `output_of`. An actor ends once: a
`finish` once it has ended, a second `finish` or one after `stop()`, fails
with `invalid_argument` and changes nothing.
. [[xactor-invariant-25]]`stop` stops an actor and its family as
`stop_child` does (<<xactor-invariant-31,guarantee 31>>) and returns the
actors it stopped; an ended one stays as it ended.
. [[xactor-invariant-26]]`output_of` is `nullptr` unless the status is
`done`; `error_of` is empty unless the status is `error`.
. [[xactor-invariant-27]]The values of `status` never change: `active` 1,
`done` 2, `error` 3, `stopped` 4.
. [[xactor-invariant-28]]The library names no message, id or error of any
user. It asks of `Message` only that it be a `std::variant` that can be
moved, whose `index()` the log records.
. [[xactor-invariant-29]]`+create_actor<Logic>(system, inputs...)+` and a
turn's `+spawn_child<Logic>(inputs...)+` construct a `Logic` from `inputs`
and run it as a new, active actor of the same system, returning its
`actor_ref`; `spawn` does the same for a logic already constructed, and
refuses a null one with `invalid_argument`. A turn of an actor that has
ended refuses `spawn_child` before it constructs anything.
. [[xactor-invariant-30]]A child spawned in a turn records the actor of
that turn as its parent, and the parent records its children in spawn
order; `turn::parent` returns the parent, and nothing for an actor the host
spawned. `turn::self` is the actor handling the message.
. [[xactor-invariant-31]]`turn::stop_child(child)` stops `child` and every
actor descended from it that is still active, each after its own
descendants, siblings in spawn order, and returns them in that order; their
mailboxes and timers are dropped, and a member that had already ended stays
as it ended while its descendants are still stopped. It is
`invalid_argument` for anything but a child of the caller (the caller
itself, a grandchild, an address that names no actor), which changes
nothing. The caller is never in the family.
. [[xactor-invariant-32]]`wake_at(deadline, message, key)` replaces the same
actor's timer under `key`; `cancel_timer(key)` removes it; keys belong to
their actor.
. [[xactor-invariant-33]]An actor that ends, done, error or stopped, loses
its timers, and an actor that is no longer active, in the rest of the turn
that ended it, arms no timer and spawns no child: `wake_at` and
`spawn_child` are `invalid_argument` and nothing is paid. `send` still
works.
. [[xactor-invariant-34]]`now()` is the time the last `clock_tick` or
`release_next` brought, 0 before any; `next_deadline()` the earliest
deadline armed, none while no timer is. A tick earlier than the last moves
`now` back: it releases what is due by then, and a deadline computed
afterwards from `now` is earlier than one computed before it.
. [[xactor-invariant-35]]`turn::stop()` ends the actor handling the message
as stopped, alone: its children are its logic's to stop, as after
`finish`. It costs nothing, the rest of the turn runs as after `finish`,
and a `stop()` once the actor has ended, a second one or one after
`finish`, is `invalid_argument`.
. [[xactor-invariant-36]]`release_next(now, correlation)` moves time to
`now` and releases the first timer due by then, as `clock_tick` would
release it, and says whether there was one; a host that runs the system
between two releases lets a timer's handling cancel or replace a later one
of the same tick.
